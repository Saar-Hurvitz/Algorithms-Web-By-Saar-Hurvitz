### Week 1 checklist

- **c1** (11 entries): I know the notations and definitions of graphs G=(V,E), |V|=n, |E|=m. Undirected graphs: degree, connected components. Directed graphs: in-degree, out-degree, strongly connected components.
- **c2** (5 entries): I'm familiar with basic graph structures (path, cycle, clique, star) and I know what a bipartite graph is.
- **c3** (3 entries): I understand and remember the Handshaking Thm in undirected graphs: Σ deg(v) = 2m. In a digraph it holds that Σ in-deg(v) = Σ out-deg(v) = m.
- **c4** (4 entries): I know the two common graph representations and understand their space complexities: 1. Adjacency Matrix O(n²), 2. Adjacency lists O(n+m).
- **c5** (3 entries): I know the definition of Topological sort and understand its meaning.
- **c6** (7 entries): I understand the proof (see recitation) that a toposort exists iff the graph is acyclic.
- **c7** (10 entries): I can find a topological sort of an acyclic graph, and I understand why it takes linear time O(n+m) when adjacency lists are used.
- **c8** (5 entries): I understand why some graphs may have several valid toposorts and I understand how the implementation of the algorithm [determines] which specific one is produced, if there are several valid toposorts.
