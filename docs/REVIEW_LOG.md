# Content review log

What has been reviewed against the slides, and what was decided. It is updated at the end of every content review (`docs/CONTENT_REVIEW.md`). Read it before a review, so that you skip what was already checked and do not re-raise what was already settled.

## How to use it

1. Run `node tools/review-fingerprints.js check`. Every item is listed as one of:
   - **UNCHANGED**: reviewed, and its source has not changed since. Skip it.
   - **CHANGED**: reviewed, but edited since. Review it again.
   - **NEW**: never reviewed with a fingerprint. Review it if it is part of this review's scope.
2. Read the **Settled decisions** of the week you are reviewing. Do not report them again unless the slide or the site has changed since.
3. After the review, the main session:
   - records the fingerprints: `node tools/review-fingerprints.js record <week> "<review title>" [extra items]`;
   - adds the review at the top of **Reviews**, and any new decisions to **Settled decisions**.

Items are written `kind:name`: `entry:w5-dag`, `term:dijkstra`, `graph:5L24`, `exercise:spByHand`, `logic:dijkstraRun`, `file:js/algorithms/sssp.js`. A week's entries, terms and graphs are recorded automatically; exercise functions, logic functions and files are listed by hand as extra items, because several weeks share those files.

Weeks 3–4 were reviewed before this log existed, so their items show as NEW. Weeks 1 and 2 were reviewed again with the review pack on 2026-11-24. They are not re-reviewed (the rule is to review only new or changed material); their outcomes are in the final reports of those weeks.
